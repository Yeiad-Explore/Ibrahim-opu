# Portfolio design direction

Reading: a one-page technical portfolio for recruiters and marketing teams, expressed through cinematic editorial typography and atmospheric depth.

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

- Color: near-black keeps the CV content authoritative; restrained cyan and coral light connect the page to the supplied reference without copying it.
- Layout: a manifesto hero, asymmetric capability composition, editorial experience list and orbital expertise panel create distinct section rhythms around the real content.
- Typography: Manrope is a precise contemporary grotesk with enough warmth for teaching and customer-facing work.
- Spacing: generous vertical space separates career chapters and lets the large display type remain readable.
- Cards: translucent panels are used only for grouped capabilities where comparison is useful; the rest of the page relies on whitespace and rules.
- Visual: one generated atmospheric artwork provides depth and geometric character without inventing a portrait or identity asset.
- Motion: restrained transforms and view reveals guide hierarchy; all movement collapses under reduced-motion preferences.
- Shape: cards use a consistent 0.9rem radius, large feature panels use a 1.2rem radius, and action buttons use a full pill shape for affordance.
